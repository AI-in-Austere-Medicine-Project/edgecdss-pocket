# EdgeCDSS Pocket work order

## Rules (owner, 2026-11-20)

1. Only signed entries speak.
2. Every change has a test. The full Pocket test suite must stay fast.
3. One PR per feature. The owner merges.
4. Ask the owner only to sign an entry or to judge something by ear on the device. Decide everything else, and tell the owner after.

## Owner rule (owner, 2026-11-23): provider levels and online medical control

Across the whole EMS list, Pocket ignores provider levels (PL) and online medical control (OLMC). It never gates a dose on them and never says them. If a drug is in the formulary, it is in the system. The signed text keeps every PL and OLMC note, for the record. What is said leaves them out (`engine/session.without_pl_olmc`, `said_indication`):
- a "Provider level:" line is not said;
- a clause naming OLMC is dropped ("Infusion over 10 min; OLMC required." becomes "Infusion over 10 min.");
- "except for OLMC approval" is cut, so the contraindication stands without it;
- a repeat that is only through OLMC is not said;
- "ALS/BLS providers" is left out of a spoken indication or quote.

Weight floors from the dose tables stay.

Pocket has no model, so it has no replays, no validator and no row-by-row rulings.

"Fast": the full suite (`python -m pytest -q`) took 105 s on 2026-11-20 (3,045 passed). A PR that makes it slower says why in its description.

The order of the sections is in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md). Device notes are in [device/RADXA.md](../device/RADXA.md).
